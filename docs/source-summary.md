# Project source summary

This summarizes the four documents supplied by the team. It is a planning reference, not proof of progress. Consult the originals and the guide for official requirements; the registration PDF is kept privately because it contains personal data.

## Registration and synopsis (FF-180)

- Group 8, IC-C, academic year 2026–27; registered project title: **A Trust-Centric Peer-to-Peer Utility and Asset Sharing Platform for Campus Communities**. The synopsis calls the project “Borrow Instead Buy”. Confirm official details with the guide.
- The intended problem is low utilization of useful student items and the expense and waste of buying them individually. The proposed solution is a campus peer-to-peer sharing platform.
- The original synopsis proposes institutional email authentication, discovery, listings, trust ratings, transactions and possible deposits and QR handovers. Later ideas include messaging, cloud deployment, lockers and UPI escrow. React/React Native, Node, Express, MongoDB and other services are proposed, not settled or implemented. The prototype now allows all email domains rather than restricting sign-up to a college address. See [the build plan](build-plan.md) for the narrower MVP.
- Claimed statistics, costs and targets in the synopsis (including zero fraud) are not established results. Verify sources and feasibility before presenting them; verified email and ratings cannot eliminate risk.
- The supplied form has blank Review 1 and Review 2 reports. The Review 1 bottom signature appears on the following page: confirm the correct submission format with the guide.

## Security and trust notes

The notes propose campus-only accounts and one account per college ID, safeguarding prices, late-return and damage handling, reviews and possible suspensions, before/after photos, and profile-level return/issue indicators. These are original ideas, not existing safeguards; the prototype now accepts all email domains rather than enforcing college-only accounts. Institutional email alone does not prove one account per person. Fair fees, dispute and appeal processes, evidence privacy and retention, and authorized access need agreement before implementation; no security measure guarantees protection from hacking. See [the build plan](build-plan.md).

## Assessment notice and preparation guide

The notice calls for an offline mid-semester review on 16 or 17 November 2026, with exact arrangements set by the guide, and assesses both group and individual work. It requires at most 13 slides, 7–8 reviewed papers, an approved Review 1 report and presentation evidence. The guide named `review-2-steps.pdf` is a preparation guide for this **mid-semester Review 1**, not an end-of-semester Review 2 notice. Its suggested work schedule and eight research starting points are guidance, not extra requirements. See [the checklist](mid-sem-review.md) and the [source documents](source-documents/README.md).
